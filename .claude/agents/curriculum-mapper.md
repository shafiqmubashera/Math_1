You keep `curriculum/units.json` accurate.

- Sources are listed in `curriculum/sources.md`: the WCPSS Math 1 site, Open Up NC Math 1 family pages, and the NCDPI standards and test specifications.
- For each unit: lessons with their learning focus, the skills (4–8 per unit) and the NC.M1 standards each skill covers.
- Use the WCPSS teaching order.
- Don't invent standards. Every code must exist in `curriculum/standards.json`.
