## Test formats used to shape questions
- **NC Check-Ins 2.0 Math 1:** 2 check-ins with 26 items each. Check-In A: A-REI.3, F-IF.2, F-IF.4, F-IF.6, G-GPE.5, S-ID.7. Check-In B: A-CED.1, A-REI.6, F-BF.1, F-IF.5, F-IF.8, F-IF.9. Item types: 4-option MC, numeric response, technology-enhanced. Calculator-inactive and calculator-active parts.
- **EOC weights:** Number & Quantity / Algebra 36–40%, Functions 32–36%, Geometry 8–12%, Statistics 18–20%.
- **Digital SAT Math:** about 75% 4-option MC and 25% student-produced response, with a built-in Desmos calculator.
